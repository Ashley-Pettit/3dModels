LOCAL NOTES (checked Nov 2026)

About Moorooka
- Southern Brisbane suburb, postcode 4105, about 7 km south of the CBD. Ipswich Road and Beaudesert Road run through it.
- Main shopping strip is on Beaudesert Road, known as Moorvale, with 100+ small businesses: cafes, takeaways, African and Middle Eastern grocers, barbers, discount stores.
- Known as Brisbane's "Little Africa". From the 2000s many families from South Sudan, Somalia, Ethiopia and Eritrea settled here. Ranchhod Arcade (197-201 Beaudesert Rd) and Moorvale Lane are the heart of it.
- Train: Moorooka station on the Beenleigh line. Nearby stations: Yeerongpilly, Rocklea, Salisbury. Buses run along Beaudesert and Ipswich Roads. Translink: 13 12 30 or translink.com.au journey planner.

Who represents Moorooka
- Local councillor: Cr Steve Griffiths (Labor), Moorooka Ward, Brisbane City Council. Councillor since 2003, re-elected 2024.
  Ward office: Shop 2, 122 Beaudesert Road, Moorooka. Phone 07 3403 1730. Email moorooka.ward@bcc.qld.gov.au. Website stevegriffiths.com.au. Hours Mon-Fri 8:45am-12:30pm and 1:30pm-4:30pm. He sends a regular e-newsletter with local events (sign up on his website or by emailing the office). You can ring or email to book a meeting.
  Moorooka Ward covers Acacia Ridge, Archerfield, Coopers Plains, Moorooka, Nathan, Rocklea, Salisbury, Willawong and parts of Annerley, Durack, Oxley and Tarragindi.
- Lord Mayor of Brisbane: Adrian Schrinner (LNP). Brisbane City Council contact centre: 07 3403 8888 (24/7) for potholes, bins, parks, dumped rubbish, dog issues.
- State MP: Moorooka is split between two state electorates: Miller (Mark Bailey, Labor) and Toohey (Peter Russo, Labor, peterrusso.au). Check which one your street is in at the Electoral Commission of Queensland (ecq.qld.gov.au).
- Federal MP: Division of Moreton, Julie-Ann Campbell (Labor), elected 2025. Office: Suite 106, Sunnybank Times Square, 250 McCullough St, Sunnybank. Phone (07) 3344 4276. Email julie-ann.campbell.mp@aph.gov.au.

Community, groups and connecting
- The Clubhouse Moorooka: not-for-profit community venue at 76 Koala Road (the former Moorooka Bowls Club). Food, drinks, bowls, trivia and bingo nights, family events, The Burbs Market. Bean on the Green cafe open 7 days 6:15am-12pm. Bar roughly Tue-Sun afternoons/evenings (check site). Membership sign-up and volunteering (gardening, marketing, events): email president@theclubhousemoorooka.org. Website theclubhousemoorooka.org.
- Moorooka Community Centre (council hall, hireable): runs free GOLD (over 50s) programs such as beginner line dancing (Mondays 11am), Chair Zumba Gold (Mondays 1:30pm) and seated ballet. Check viabrisbane.com or brisbane.qld.gov.au events for current dates.
- Moorooka Greening Group: volunteers restoring habitat at Mayfield Gardens, 311 Tarragindi Road.
- Annerley-Moorooka Senior Citizens, a Scout den (Clifton Hall Scouts), St John Ambulance and the Palm and Cycad Societies all use park facilities in Moorooka.
- Moorooka Block Party: big free world-music and culture festival at Pegg's Park, part of Brisbane Festival every September. 2026 was its fifth year (Sat 12 Sep 2026, 3-7pm).
- Moorooka State School holds a fete each July. Schools: Moorooka State School (Beaudesert Rd), St Brendan's Catholic Primary (Hawtree St).
- Churches: St Brendan's Catholic Church (Hawtree St), Church of Christ (Pampas St and Beaudesert Rd).
- Libraries (no library in Moorooka itself): Coopers Plains Library, 107 Orange Grove Rd, 07 3403 1530 (Tue, Thu, Fri 10am-5pm; Wed 10am-8pm; Sat 9am-4pm; closed Sun, Mon, public holidays). Annerley Library, 450 Ipswich Rd, 07 3403 1735 (it closed for repairs after ex-Cyclone Alfred in 2025, so check it's open).

Food
- Yeshi Buna Ethio-African Cafe & Restaurant, 131 Beaudesert Rd: Ethiopian food and traditional coffee ceremony; a local favourite.
- Arhibu Ethiopian Restaurant: in the basement of Ranchhod Arcade; breakfast, lunch and dinner.
- Afro Restaurant: Ethiopian/Eritrean, chef's platters with vegan options.
- Salina Eritrean Cafe: Moorvale Lane, casual and good value.
- Vizzeto: small woodfired pizzeria (Brazilian/Italian/Aussie fusion).
- Also: a South African grocer and butcher (biltong, boerewors) on Beaudesert Rd, a Middle Eastern and Afghan grocer in the 1950s Hughes Centre, and one of Brisbane's oldest Chinese restaurants.

Parks and outdoors
- Pegg's Park (Vendale Ave / Koala Rd): playground, home of the Block Party. Named after Pegg's Farm from the late 1800s.
- Evans Road Park: dog off-leash area with a separate small-dog enclosure.
- Alexander Park (Moorooka Recreation Reserve), 334 Beaudesert Rd: AFL ground and playground.
- Veterans Park, 48 Anson St: picnic shelter and outdoor fitness equipment.
- Luxworth Place, 135 Beaudesert Rd: small plaza with stage, toilets and drinking fountain.
- Stimpson Park, 904 Ipswich Rd: memorial.
- Tennessee Avenue Park and Feldt Street Park (333 Tarragindi Rd): playgrounds.
- Toohey Forest (next door in Tarragindi/Nathan): bushwalking and mountain-bike trails.
