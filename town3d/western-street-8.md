# 8 Western Street

The [26 March 2022 Esri World Imagery view](https://www.arcgis.com/apps/mapviewer/index.html?layers=10df2279f9684e4a9f6a7f08febac2a9&center=145.720933349,-38.638517457&level=20), Vantor scene 6072228 at 0.50 m, shows the multipart pale roof south of No.6. The 10 cm historical Vicmap view corroborates the western solar array, the recessed south wall, and the eastern roof return. Its overlapping metadata name December 2021 and January 2023 projects. Vicmap data © State of Victoria, CC BY 4.0. Roof junction heights, joinery and colors are representative. The imagery is used as placement evidence and no reference pixels ship in Explore.

The two same-title procedural shells 4058 and 4059 and the seeded trunk 40307 within the eastern wall are masked. The native road, own and neighboring painted approaches, and adjacent buildings are preserved. The original painter supplies the two pad corrections in one detail patch with two quality variants. The former north spur remains visible as an approximation because the available aerial does not establish a complete paved boundary. Neighboring parcels at Nos.4 and 6 stand outside the solid and ground edits.
