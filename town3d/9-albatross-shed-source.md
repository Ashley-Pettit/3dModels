# Detached shed at 9 Albatross Drive

The January 2023 Alex Scott & Staff campaign identifies a separate shed of approximately 6 by 3.4 m at No. 9. Its rear aerial shows the pale structure just inside the fence, separate from the house and attached garage. Esri World Imagery shows the same independent roof on 13 May 2025. The roof corners were sampled in the authorized map viewer. The agent media corroborates the use, approximate dimensions and pale finish. No source image is shipped.
