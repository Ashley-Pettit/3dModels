# 48 Lohr Avenue

The [Microsoft Global ML Building Footprints](https://github.com/microsoft/GlobalMLBuildingFootprints) roof microsoft-311223133-b58329e02033e68c, licensed under [CDLA-Permissive-2.0](https://cdla.dev/permissive-2-0/), lies inside [Vicmap Property](https://discover.data.vic.gov.au/en_AU/dataset/vicmap-property-parcel-polygon-with-parcel-detail) parcel 5283365, Lot 90 LP051833, at active [Vicmap Address](https://discover.data.vic.gov.au/en_AU/dataset/vicmap-address-address-point) PFI 53198981. Vicmap data © State of Victoria, CC BY 4.0. [Historical Vicmap Basemaps](https://www.land.vic.gov.au/maps-and-spatial/spatial-data/vicmap-basemaps) and the [13 May 2025 Vantor scene](https://www.arcgis.com/apps/mapviewer/index.html?layers=10df2279f9684e4a9f6a7f08febac2a9&center=145.70009023,-38.64537578&level=20) show the same principal roof. The [October 2025 street view](https://www.google.com/maps/@?api=1&map_action=pano&pano=glY82xruoDJ6BJ5wEn9AGQ&heading=180&pitch=0) confirms a raised pale frontage with a balcony, central steps, a smaller upper storey and a closed garage at the east end.

One vertex-coloured mesh recreates the raised house, covered deck, simple rail and the upper storey. The 11.54 m² garage is the enclosed ground-level portion supported by the street view and receives a ground collider. Room and stair details are approximate. The west path and adjoining houses are preserved. Two generated tree centres under the confirmed roof are hidden and the three sampled surrounding centres remain. The existing ground texture still represents the route to the garage.
