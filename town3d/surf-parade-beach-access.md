# Surf Parade beach access 38

The visual upgrade covers one timber landing and stair at the east end of Surf Parade, near the foreshore car park between Ayr Creek and Veronica Street. The native concrete approach and shared path already follow the mapped route. The new timber geometry fits their existing landings, with handrails that follow the stair and a clear walking channel down the centre. The timber finish, rail dimensions and step count are estimates.

The geometry uses one host mesh with vertex colours. It ships without source photographs or new texture requests. Stage 3a, Veronica Street to Ozone Street, [opened in June 2022](https://engage.basscoast.vic.gov.au/surfpde/3a). Stage 3b, Ozone Street to Goroke Street, [was under construction on 18 November 2026](https://www.sgst.com.au/finished-by-christmas-december-deadline-for-inverloch-shared-pathway/).
