# Driftwood Cottage at 83 Toorak Road

The house is on Vicmap Lot 1 LP209234, parcel PFI 5285208. The 13 May 2025 Vantor image shows the cottage running north–south, with a contiguous paler roof at the rear. A 2016 sale photo identifies the cottage by name and street number, and shows pale weatherboards, dark red trim, a metal roof and a separate front footpath. The model uses an approximately 157.6 m² main roof and conservatively inset 128.5 m² of enclosed walls. The roof height and pitch are estimates. Two narrow visual posts depict approximate support for the rear cover. The opening remains clear because the wall arrangement could not be verified. [Dated aerial](https://www.arcgis.com/apps/mapviewer/index.html?layers=10df2279f9684e4a9f6a7f08febac2a9&center=145.708390,-38.636641&level=20) · [Sale photo archive](https://www.property.com.au/vic/inverloch-3996/toorak-rd/83-pid-4527964/)

The new cottage replaces displaced stock slots 243 and 3860. Their centres identify the intended parcel, and both clash with the actual cottage. The older east–west shell extended into No. 81 but touched only the outer edge of its roof in the aerial. No. 81's existing model, frontage, trees and access are retained. The one masked tree trunk was seeded inside the surveyed main walls. The model leaves the foot approach, public path, adjoining road and neighboring parcel available. The two generated driveways remain, including the section of the old Tamara Link spur outside the title.
