# 10 Toorak Road

The [13 May 2025 Vantor aerial](https://www.arcgis.com/apps/mapviewer/index.html?layers=10df2279f9684e4a9f6a7f08febac2a9&center=145.71763508,-38.63704608&level=20) (World Imagery scene 6072283) and the [historical Vicmap mosaic](https://www.land.vic.gov.au/maps-and-spatial/spatial-data/vicmap-basemaps) show a compact blue-grey hipped roof behind a substantial screen of trees. The older mosaic has overlapping acquisition metadata for December 2021 and January 2023. The [October 2025 street view](https://www.google.com/maps/@?api=1&map_action=pano&pano=uq3XurbibdbodvYqSDpcYg&heading=0&pitch=4&fov=80) shows the exposed upper window, warm cladding and olive lower bay behind the gravel crossover. Foliage largely hides the rest of the façade. [Vicmap property data](https://discover.data.vic.gov.au/en_AU/dataset/vicmap-property-parcel-polygon-with-parcel-detail) place the house within active parcel PFI 5287161 and are © State of Victoria, CC BY 4.0.

The [Microsoft footprint dataset](https://github.com/microsoft/GlobalMLBuildingFootprints) (microsoft-311223133-943b5f204ec06e99, [CDLA-Permissive-2.0](https://cdla.dev/permissive-2-0/)) supplies the plan. The 0.45 m east fit follows the older visible west eave and keeps the 103.6 m² roof more than 0.45 m inside the parcel. The 86.6 m² enclosed collider replaces two same-title procedural shells. One shell wrongly stands in the tree-covered front setback. The second overlaps the main roof. The existing ground, vegetation and neighboring houses are retained. The visible gravel crossover, gate and mature verge tree are follow-up frontage work. Image pixels are used only as visual evidence.
