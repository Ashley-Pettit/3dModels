## 71 Cuttriss Street house and open carport

The [23 February 2025 Vantor scene 6072276 in Esri World Imagery](https://www.arcgis.com/apps/mapviewer/index.html?layers=10df2279f9684e4a9f6a7f08febac2a9&center=145.740589,-38.627088&level=20) shows one pale main roof, a lower southeast cover and the approach from Cuttriss Street on active [Vicmap Property](https://discover.data.vic.gov.au/en_AU/dataset/vicmap-property-parcel-polygon-with-parcel-detail) parcel PFI 5284297, address PFI 53199555. Vicmap is © State of Victoria, CC BY 4.0. The [manual roof witnesses](../tools/streetscapes/71-cuttriss-street-2025-02-23-roof-witnesses.geojson) use the 0.31 m aerial with 5 m positional accuracy. [Stockdale & Leggo's archived frontage photograph](https://www.domain.com.au/property-profile/71-cuttriss-street-inverloch-vic-3996) corroborates white cladding, green posts and the open carport. Wall height, roof pitch and post positions are estimated.

One untextured mesh supplies a 72.4 m² closed wall ring and a 16.3 m² open cover. Only the house and four carport posts block ground-level movement. The two legacy shells crossed into the parcel at No. 69 by 11.3 m² and 11.0 m². Centre masks remove superseded slots 1055 and 1056, including the oversized stock block over the carport and the shed in the west yard. No. 69's separate models and the existing terrain and driveway paint are preserved.
