# Ayr Creek bank beside 26 Beacon Court

The 13 May 2025 Vantor scene shows the pale roof of the residence west of the inherited model's creek-bank shell. At the inherited shell's centre the image shows continuous mature vegetation. The 2026 seller documentation identifies a 119 m² residence and two separate sheds, all within the private plot. The older generated bank shell has no corresponding building in that evidence. It covers 105.04 m², of which 15.40 m² is in Lot 257 LP10678 and the remaining 89.64 m² is outside all active parcels returned there. The overlay removes slot 4141 and its collision. [Esri World Imagery](https://www.arcgis.com/apps/mapviewer/index.html?layers=10df2279f9684e4a9f6a7f08febac2a9&center=145.716431,-38.639139&level=20) · [Seller’s site and plan](https://www.26beaconcourt.com/)

The existing representation of the private buildings, gardens, driveway and creek path stays in place. The unrelated nearest model centre is 9.19 m from the masked point, and the trail edge is 3.82 m from the old footprint. The canopy still obscures parts of the existing buildings. This change uses one centre mask and keeps the existing terrain and landscape.
